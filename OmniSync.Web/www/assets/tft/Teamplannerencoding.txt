Updated Technical Specification: Chunked ID Serialization

For developers building parsers, the logic should follow this schema:

    Header (2 chars): 02 (Signals protocol version 2).

    Payload (30 chars): 10 slots of 3-character hexadecimal IDs.

        Slot 1: Chars 3–5

        Slot 2: Chars 6–8

        ...and so on.

    Padding: Unused slots must be filled with 000.

    Trailer (9 chars): TFTSet16.
