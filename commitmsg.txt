fix(android): ensure sleep tracking persists through app restarts and non-user activity

- Refactored MainViewModel.recordActivity to update 'last active' timestamp without automatically resetting sleep state.
- Added recordUserActivity for explicit manual activity that should reset sleep.
- Removed recordActivity call from MainActivity.onCreate to prevent accidental sleep reset on app restart (especially background restarts).
- This ensures sleep tracking continues until the user explicitly wakes up or significant activity is detected.
