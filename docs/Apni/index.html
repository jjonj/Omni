The interaction contract

Hold opens at pointer position. Aim through a category to expose its outer actions or grouped windows. Release confirms the highlighted target. Release at the center or outside the wheel, or press Escape, to dismiss safely. Click the sample desktop to place the menu at a new cursor position. In the preview, button taps also demonstrate selections.

  • Unified: Apps, Scripts, OmniSweep, Workspaces, Tools and System are first-ring categories. Hover category to open second-ring actions.
  • Separate: Launch wheel retains the same categories; Windows is a fast dedicated wheel, grouped by application with simulated live-window previews.
  • Add Mode: copper dashed + gaps are insertion targets between categories and between nested tiles. Empty category/wheel presets show the first-item experience; click a ghost to add.
  • Edit in-wheel: select a tile, rename it, change its kind or reorder it without leaving the overlay. Changes stay inside this prototype only.

Design gate: no Windows hooks, real window captures, app launches, OmniSweep mutations, services, or production wheel are implemented here.
